# P13 — Advanced constraints, pose grids and deformation controls

[Roadmap](../README.md) · [All phases](../PHASES.md)

> Generated from `roadmap.json`; edit the canonical JSON and regenerate.

Build sophisticated reusable character controls and layered deformation rigs.

**Status:** planned. **Dependencies:** P08, P09, P10. **Effort:** 14–28 focused engineer-weeks.

**Milestone:** Advanced character animation.

## Work packages

### P13-W1

Extend the core two-bone IK solver to multi-chain constraints, nails and animated constraint transitions with explicit solve order; add the Breakdown Assistant.

### P13-W2

Add free-form, shape-aware and weighted deformations plus deform-on-deform composition; evaluate selected Eigen/libigl solvers.

### P13-W3

Extend declarative controllers with two-dimensional pose grids and deformer-on-deformer composition; custom scripting uses P12, while ordinary widgets/sliders/dashboard/templates are owned by P08.

## Exit criteria

- Complete WF-06 with a 60-control rig, mirrored poses and combined substitutions/deformations.
- Test unreachable IK targets and degenerate weighted meshes without NaNs or corrupted saved data.
- Controller mappings round-trip through templates and behave consistently with direct channel editing.
- Measure solve cost during interaction and final render; document approximation and influence limits per solver.
